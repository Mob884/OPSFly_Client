Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Welcome to OpsFly ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web-based administration dashboard (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use the Service. The information we may collect includes:

A. Personal Data

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, password hashes, and organization/department associations when you register or are invited to the platform.
  • Profile Details: Your role inside the organization (e.g., Owner, District Manager, General Manager, Department Manager).

B. Audio and Voice Recording Data

  • Voice Notes: When you use the voice dictation feature to record operational logs, our Service accesses your device's microphone to capture the audio file. These audio recordings are transmitted to our servers for AI-powered transcription.

C. Operational Logs and Task Information

  • Audit Notes: Written text notes, audit logs, and transcriptions of recorded voice notes.
  • Task Data: Details of tasks created, completion statuses, assignees, priorities, and resolution times.
  • Location Scoping: Association of notes and tasks to specific branch locations within your organization.

D. Device and Notification Data

  • Push Notification Tokens: Unique Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens associated with your device to send task assignments, critical alerts, and system updates.
  • Device Details: Basic operating system parameters (iOS or Android) used to ensure app performance and deliver correct notification configurations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to operate, maintain, and improve our services, including to:

  • Create and secure your user account.
  • Transcribe voice recordings into text logs.
  • Analyze operational notes using Artificial Intelligence to detect issues, categorize concerns, and suggest operational remedies.
  • Distribute and coordinate tasks and assignments among organization team members.
  • Send push notifications regarding urgent issues, updates, and assignments.
  • Export performance audits and daily summaries to CSV logs.
  • Improve AI analysis patterns, system diagnostics, and application speed.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data. We share your data only with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, subject to strict confidentiality agreements:

Third-Party Processors:
  • OpenAI API: Used to process and transcribe raw voice audio files into text notes.
  • Google Gemini & OpenRouter APIs: Used to analyze text notes for operational issues, severity categorization, and recommendation actions.
  • Google Firebase (FCM): Used to coordinate and push real-time system alerts to mobile clients.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Secure cloud storage provider hosting active database records.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. Active sessions are protected via industry-standard JSON Web Token (JWT) authorization. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using advanced cloud storage protocols.

While we take reasonable steps to secure your personal information, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against intercept or misuse.

5. Your Rights and Choices

A. Microphone Access

You can grant or revoke microphone permissions at any time through your mobile device settings or browser settings. Revoking permission will disable the voice recording feature, but you will still be able to input text manually.

B. Push Notifications

You can choose to opt-out of push notifications at any time by updating your system settings on your iOS or Android device.

C. Access, Rectification, and Deletion

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request the deletion of your account and associated data. Organization owners can delete locations and team rosters from the administration settings dashboard.

To request complete account deletion, please contact us at the email listed below.
